I am a research intern in the Computational Brain & Intelligence Laboratory at KIST.
My interests lie at the intersection of neuroscience and artificial intelligence.
I want to understand the neural computations underlying learning and information
processing, and explore what they can teach us about artificial intelligence.

During my master's research, I built biophysical models of auditory and visual
cortical circuits to study how disinhibition shapes information transmission
across sensory pathways. I have also worked on safety alignment for medical
large language models, developing a method that constructs domain-specific
preference data without new domain annotations or external judges.
