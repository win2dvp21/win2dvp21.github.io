Seungyoun Lee

Research Intern, Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST)

Email · GitHub · LinkedIn

I completed my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Korea University and now work as a research intern in the Computational Brain & Intelligence Laboratory at KIST. My interests lie at the intersection of neuroscience and artificial intelligence. I want to understand the neural computations underlying learning and information processing, and explore what they can teach us about artificial intelligence.

My master’s research used biophysical models of auditory and visual cortical circuits to study how disinhibition shapes information transmission. I have also worked on safety alignment for medical large language models.
